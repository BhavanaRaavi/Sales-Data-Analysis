Sales Data Analysis Project

Key Business Insights

1️⃣ Technology category generates the highest profit margins
Products like Copiers and Phones are strong profit drivers.

2️⃣ Tables, Bookcases, and Supplies are loss-making sub-categories
Even though Tables have high sales, they generate negative profit.

3️⃣ Discounts above ~20% significantly reduce profit margin
Heavy discounting leads to losses in several products.

4️⃣ Sales and profits peak in the last quarter (Nov–Dec)
This indicates strong seasonality in demand.

5️⃣ A small number of products generate a large portion of revenue
Example: Canon imageCLASS copier dominates product sales.

6️⃣ High sales does not always mean high profit
Some products sell a lot but generate poor margins.
